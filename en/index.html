01 / THE CONCEPT

Two lumens.
One purposeful design.

When a procedure shares oral access, every detail of the device matters. Explore the architecture behind OROPHAR technology.

OROPHAR / DUAL-LUMEN DESIGN
Reference image • previous generation01 / 01

The photograph shows the previous generation. Confirm supplied-model features in the current instructions for use.

01

Separate access pathways

Two lumens organize oral access: an instrument passage and a separate airway-related channel.

02

Oxygen and capnography

The design includes connections for O₂ delivery and exhaled CO₂ sampling, bringing functions into one device.

03

An interface for instruments

A bite block and a dedicated passage form an architecture designed to accommodate instruments during the procedure.

Concept description based on the Brazilian patent. A patent does not replace product indications or instructions for use. View technical design↗

Read the findings in context.

Mild desaturation occurred in 7/30 patients and laryngospasm in 1/30. Airway alignment maneuvers were needed in 28/30. No severe desaturation occurred.

This study had no comparator group and used the previous generation (ANVISA 80423540061). It is not clinical validation specific to OROPHAR 2.0 or proof of superiority over Guedel.
