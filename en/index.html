The device

More access. More control. Fewer interruptions.

Where a conventional airway offers a single channel, separates the airway from the instrument passage and builds oxygen and capnography into the device itself.

Double lumen

A round opening for upper GI endoscopy probes and a square opening for flexible bronchofibroscopy.

Continuous oxygen

A dedicated extension delivers O₂ throughout the procedure, with no improvisation.

Built-in capnography

A second extension connects to the capnograph to monitor expired CO₂ in real time.

Protection and comfort

A bite block that protects the optical fiber, an anatomical shape with rounded edges and an elastic securing strap.

Conventional Guedel airway

  • Single central lumen
  • Competes with the endoscopic probe for the oral route
  • No integrated O₂ or CO₂ connections

  • Two lumens with distinct functions
  • Exclusive instrument passage, airway preserved
  • Oxygen and capnography lines built into the device

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is ?

A double-lumen oropharyngeal airway, single-use and supplied sterile. It keeps the airway open during sedation and lets you pass the endoscopic probe, deliver oxygen and monitor CO₂ with a single device.

Which procedures can it be used in?

Upper GI endoscopy, flexible bronchofibroscopy, transesophageal echocardiography, therapeutic gastroduodenal procedures such as ERCP and sedation with non-invasive ventilation without intubation. It can also guide orotracheal tubes in emergencies.

Who is it indicated for?

Adult patients under sedation with spontaneous ventilation, in a hospital setting and by a qualified physician. Always follow the instructions for use.
