INFORMATIONAL OVERVIEW

OROPHAR 2.0

Dual-lumen oropharyngeal cannula

Concept

An architecture with two lumens, an instrument passage and connections intended for oxygen delivery and CO₂ sampling. The concept description is based on patent BR102019003396B1.

Identification references

  • ANVISA: 82249100019 · Class I notification
  • Process: 25351.002269/2024-57
  • Reported sizes: 80, 90, 100 mm
  • Company named in the 2024 publication: O-LINKE MEDICAL LTDA.

Published evidence

Nigro Neto C. et al. New oropharyngeal double lumen cannula for sedation for transesophageal echocardiography: case series. Brazilian Journal of Anesthesiology. 2020;70(2):134–139. DOI: 10.1016/j.bjane.2020.04.019.

A series of 30 adults with no comparator group, using the previous OROPHAR generation. It does not constitute validation specific to version 2.0.
